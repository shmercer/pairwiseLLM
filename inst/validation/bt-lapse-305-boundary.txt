Implementation and numerical investigation
  The likelihood remains (1-epsilon) * plogis(theta_A-theta_B+beta) + epsilon/2.
  Centering, model variant btl_e_b, positional interpretation, and no-penalty
  estimation are unchanged. Natural epsilon in [0,1] replaces logit-epsilon
  optimization, avoiding the vanishing transformed score near zero. L-BFGS-B
  retains the same five starts and iteration limit; factr is reltol divided by
  machine precision, and pgtol is gradient_tol divided by comparison count.
  At most ten damped natural-coordinate Newton steps polish native successes.
  No score, step, condition-number, objective-tie, or recovery tolerance changed.

  Epsilon zero is optimized separately with BFGS. Its theta/beta convergence,
  stationarity and curvature are checked even for an interior fit. A boundary
  point also requires the one-sided negative-log-likelihood epsilon score to be
  >= -gradient_tol and full-model expected-information identification. A strict
  positive score requires curvature along the theta/beta face; when that score
  is within gradient_tol of zero, full joint observed curvature must also pass.
  Epsilon one remains unidentified. Invalid candidates retain typed failures.

  The exact-zero optimum may win or tie the best joint objective within the
  original objective tolerance, but only if KKT holds. A deterministic positive
  lapse case at 1e-7 verifies that an objective tie with a negative boundary
  score still returns an interior fit. Candidate choice never substitutes a
  materially worse likelihood to obtain uncertainty.

  The first follow-up audit returned 351 interior and 98 boundary fits, with
  one information-matrix rejection and no recovery failures among valid fits.
  At the rejected 8-item sparse population case (beta 0.3, epsilon 0.2), the
  weighted two-argument cross-product had asymmetry of 1.136868e-13 near a true
  zero entry. The existing relative symmetry check correctly rejected it.
  Computing expected information as crossprod(sqrt(weight) * derivative)
  evaluates the identical formula with computational symmetry. No tolerance,
  eigenvalue, covariance repair, or model change was used. An independent
  information oracle and exact-symmetry regression cover this calculation.

  That first audit also emitted six invalid-log warnings from out-of-domain
  trial evaluations. Such trials now abort and retain an explicit failed-attempt
  message before evaluating the likelihood; epsilon is never clipped. The final
  audit emitted zero warnings. Seven of the 2,250 joint starts in seven cases
  were unsuccessful; all attempts remain in fit diagnostics and per-case failed
  counts are in the CSV. Final fit validity is not a claim that every start
  converged, or that all possible modes were found.
